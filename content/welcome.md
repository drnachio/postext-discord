# Welcome to Postext 👋

**A programmable typesetter for the web.**

Postext is an open-source (MIT) layout engine that brings centuries of print typesetting craft to modern web technology. You write semantic markdown; Postext lays it out like a professional typesetter would, with Knuth-Plass justification, hyphenation, orphan and widow control, float placement, cross-references and LaTeX math. Output goes to **HTML**, **canvas** or **print-ready PDF** (CMYK and PDF/UA).

This is the home of the Postext community. It's for **publishers and editorial teams**, **developers** building with Postext, and **contributors** of every kind: code, typography, design, docs and translations.
