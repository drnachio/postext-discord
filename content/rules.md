# 📜 Community Guidelines

Postext brings together publishers, developers, typographers and designers. These guidelines keep this a place where everyone can learn from each other. By taking part in this server, you agree to follow them, along with Discord's [Terms of Service](<https://discord.com/terms>) and [Community Guidelines](<https://discord.com/guidelines>).

**1. Be kind and respectful.**
Our [Code of Conduct](<{link:github}/blob/develop/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>) (Contributor Covenant) applies here. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or trolling. Critique ideas, not people. Everyone was a beginner once.

**2. Use the right channel.**
Questions go in {#help} (one post per question). Ideas go in {#feature-requests}. Publishing needs go in {#publisher-desk}. Keep other channels on topic, and use {#off-topic} for everything else.

**3. Ask good questions.**
Say which Postext version and environment you're using (browser, Node.js, worker). Include a *minimal* markdown and config snippet in a code block, plus what you expected and what happened. Please don't cross-post, and don't ping or DM maintainers or members for support.

**4. Code and decisions live on GitHub.**
Discord is for conversation. Confirmed bugs become [Issues](<{link:github}/issues>). Design proposals go to [Discussions](<{link:github}/discussions>). Before writing code, **open an issue first** to agree on the approach. This keeps every decision public and searchable.
<!-- split -->
**5. Never report security issues in public.**
Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](<{link:github}/security/advisories/new>) or follow [SECURITY.md](<{link:github}/blob/develop/SECURITY.md>).

**6. Respect confidentiality and rights.**
Don't share unpublished manuscripts, confidential client material, or content you don't have the rights to. Use sample or placeholder text in reproductions. Respect font licences and copyright.

**7. No spam or unsolicited promotion.**
Don't post invite links, send unsolicited DMs, spam ads or job posts, or post the same thing again and again. Sharing work you made *with Postext* in {#showcase} is very welcome.

**8. English in public channels.**
English is the common language here, so everyone can follow along. Feel free to add a short note in your own language if it helps.

**9. Be transparent about AI-generated content.**
AI tools are welcome, and Postext even ships an agent skill. Still, check AI-generated answers and code before you post them, and say so when a large part of a post was generated.

**10. Keep it safe for work.**
No NSFW, violent or shocking content, anywhere, including avatars and usernames.

## 🛡️ Moderation

Moderators may edit or remove content and act on behaviour that breaks these guidelines. Usually that means a **warning**, then a **timeout**, then a **ban**. Severe cases can skip steps.

**See something wrong?** Mention {@moderator}, DM a moderator, or use Discord's *Report* feature. All reports are handled confidentially.

*These guidelines may evolve with the community. Significant changes will be announced in {#announcements}.*
