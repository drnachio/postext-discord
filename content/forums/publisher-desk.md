**We build Postext for real editorial production, and your input shapes the roadmap.** A useful post covers:
```
**Who we are:** Publisher / imprint / team (as much as you want to share)
**What we publish:** Books, journals, magazines, textbooks... formats & volume
**How we produce it today:** Tools and workflow (InDesign, LaTeX, XML/EPUB pipelines...)
**Where it hurts:** What's slow, manual, expensive or impossible
**What we'd need from Postext:** Features, output requirements (PDF/X, CMYK, PDF/UA...)
**Sample pages:** Photos or PDFs of layouts you'd like to reproduce (non-confidential)
```
🤝 **Interested in a pilot?** Tag your post *Pilot interest* and a maintainer will get in touch.
🖨️ Technical print questions are also welcome in {#print-production}.

Maintainers add *Acknowledged*, *Planned* or *Shipped* tags so you can follow what happens with your request.
