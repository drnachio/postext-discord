# 📚 Welcome, publishers & editorial teams

Postext exists because the web never learned what typesetters have known for centuries: balanced columns, no widows or orphans, figures placed where they belong, and justification without rivers. We want Postext to fit **real editorial production**, and that means hearing from you.

## How we can help each other
📝 **Tell us about your publication** in {#publisher-desk}: what you publish, how you produce it today, and where it hurts. One post per publication or need.
🖨️ **Print-ready output**: CMYK PDF, spot colours, PDF/UA accessibility and printer handoff are discussed in {#print-production}.
🔄 **Workflows**: moving from manuscript to page, single-source print + web, multilingual editions, and AI-assisted editing (see the tutorials in {#videos}).
🤝 **Pilots**: if you'd like to try Postext on a real title or series, tag your post *Pilot interest*.

## A note on confidentiality
This is a public server. Please don't share unpublished manuscripts or client material. Use sample pages or placeholder text. If you need to discuss something privately, ask a {@moderator} and we'll find the right channel.
